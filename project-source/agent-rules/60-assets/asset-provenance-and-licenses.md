# Asset Provenance and Licenses

Third-party production assets remain legally traceable, and their modification and distribution rights match the intended use.

## Provenance

- **[MUST]** Every third-party production asset or package has a traceable source and evidence of its license or permission.
- **[MUST]** "Free" or "royalty-free" alone is not a license conclusion. Verify the terms for modification, redistribution, attribution, commercial use and any other applicable condition.
- **[MUST]** Ambiguous permission is not accepted for production distribution until it is resolved.
- **[MUST]** Store provenance at the unit of authorization that makes sense: per package, per asset or in a central manifest. Do not mechanically require one license file per PNG.
- **[MUST]** Modify a third-party asset only within its actual license permission, and keep the required attribution and source notices.
