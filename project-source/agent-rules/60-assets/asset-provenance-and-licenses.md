# Asset Provenance and Licenses

Third-party production assets must remain legally traceable and modification/distribution rights must match intended use.

## Provenance

- **[MUST]** Every third-party production asset/package has traceable source and license/permission evidence.
- **[MUST]** `free` or `royalty-free` alone is not a license conclusion. Verify modification, redistribution, attribution, commercial-use, and other applicable terms.
- **[MUST]** Ambiguous permission is not accepted for production distribution until resolved.
- **[MUST]** Store provenance at the authorization unit that makes sense: package-level, per-asset, or central manifest. Do not mechanically require one license file per PNG.
- **[MUST]** Third-party modification remains within actual license permission and keeps required attribution/source notices.
