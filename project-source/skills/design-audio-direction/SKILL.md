# Sound Bible

Follow `methodology/11-specialist-workflows.md`. Read the relevant experience
goals, feedback behavior, existing audio conventions and available references.

Maintain `docs/audio/sound-bible.md` for current shared decisions: mood and sonic
identity; roles of music, ambience and effects; importance and coexistence of
feedback; transitions, repetition fatigue and silence; accessibility needs such
as whether essential information also has a visual channel. Scope each rule to
its applicable scenes or sound families.

Describe useful timbre, texture, rhythmic and dynamic characteristics in terms
that guide a concrete specification. Do not invent loudness targets, sample rates,
technical bus topology or a complete soundtrack when they are not requirements.
Proposed behavior changes return to Product; project mixing implementation belongs
to the authorized task.

References establish direction only; written descriptions are not listening
evidence and no generated examples are promised. Music and SFX production remain
reserved capabilities. Use `design-audio-asset-spec` for concrete deliverables and
`content-asset-integration` for supplied files. Formal evaluation uses `review-audio`
in a different instance. Report confirmed direction, applicable sources and gaps.
